## SECTION: Introduction
Main article: Wizards Istar (pl. Istari ) A Quenya word meaning approximately " Wise One ", translated as " Wizard ", or "those who know".

## SECTION: Etymology
The word comes from the verb ista- ("to know"), and possibly the agentive ending -ro .

## SECTION: Other forms
Istarion : "of Wizards" ( genitive plural )

## SECTION: Cognates
Sindarin ithron

## SECTION: Examples
Heren Istarion Istar , the original, discarded, name of Tar-Míriel
