Sauron was a powerful Maia who became the principal servant of Morgoth during the First Age.

After Morgoth's defeat, Sauron eventually rose as the Dark Lord of Middle-earth.

Sauron created the One Ring in order to dominate the other Rings of Power. His power became closely tied to the Ring.

He established Mordor as his principal realm and constructed the fortress of Barad-dur.

During the War of the Last Alliance, Sauron was defeated and the One Ring was taken from him by Isildur.

Thousands of years later, Sauron returned to power during the Third Age. His final defeat came when the One Ring was destroyed in Mount Doom.
